# KM-Reader Studio

**An offline-first academic PDF reader & automated Literature Review Synthesis Matrix workspace.**  
Built for postgraduate students, researchers, and lecturers who want to extract structured literature reviews in seconds—with **100% local data privacy** and **zero login walls**.

## What It Does

* **1-Click Auto Matrix:** Automatically extracts a 5-pillar, 10-dimension Literature Review Matrix (*Context & Setting, Research Objective, Theoretical Framework, Study Design, Data Analysis, Main Results, Conclusions, Strengths & Limitations, and Relevance*).
* **Sentence-Anchored Jump Links (`[1]–[10]`):** Every extracted claim in your matrix includes clickable `[1]–[10]` badges that jump straight to and highlight the exact source sentence inside your PDF.
* **Cross-Paper Synthesis:** Compare consensus, contradictions, and research gaps across multiple studies and generate a draft literature review paragraph.
* **Export & Merge Anytime:** Save portable `.json` backups to merge across devices, or export clean **CSV** and **A4 Landscape PDF** tables.
* **100% Local-First Privacy:** All PDFs, highlights, sticky notes, and matrices stay inside your own device's storage (`IndexedDB` & `localStorage`). Nothing is uploaded to any external server.
