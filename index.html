How to Start — KM-Reader Studio

Set up your free AI engine first, then extract your Literature Matrix

Step 1 — Get & Paste Your Free Gemini API Key

Click Gemini 3.5 in the top bar → click Get Free API Key ↗ on Google AI Studio → paste your key and click Save Settings. (Stored locally in your browser only.)

Step 2 — Open Your Academic PDFs

Click + Open in the top bar to load one or multiple research PDFs, or try the built-in Sample Paper.

Step 3 — Click "1-Click Auto Matrix" & Verify [1]–[10]

Press Auto Matrix on the second toolbar to generate your 5-pillar synthesis table. Click any [1] badge to jump straight to the highlighted sentence in the PDF.

Step 4 — Export to BibTeX, CSV, or Landscape PDF

Send citations straight to Zotero/Mendeley (BibTeX), Excel (CSV), or a printable PDF.

About & Data Privacy

KM-Reader Studio v19 — Local-First Research Workspace

100% Local File Processing

All PDFs, highlights, sticky notes, and Literature Matrix tables are processed and stored locally inside your browser/desktop storage (IndexedDB & localStorage). Your manuscripts are never uploaded to any third-party cloud server.

Direct Gemini API Connection (BYOK)

When you configure your own Google AI Studio API key, text excerpts are sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API over HTTPS. Offline matrix extraction works completely without an internet connection.
